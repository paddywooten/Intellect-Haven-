Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information

Introduction

At Intellect Haven, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

We are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and other applicable privacy laws.

Key Principles

We collect only what we need, use it transparently, protect it securely, and never sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you provide directly to us:

Category Examples
Account Information Name, email, phone number, password, profile photo
Parent Information Children's names, ages, schools, learning needs
Teacher Information Qualifications, experience, subjects, availability, hourly rates
Verification Documents ID cards, certificates, reference letters (for teachers)
Payment Information Mobile Money numbers (processed securely by Paystack)
Communications Messages, reviews, support requests

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Platform, we automatically collect:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent, click patterns
  • Location Data: General location based on IP address (city level)
  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device (see Cookies section)

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

  • Payment processors (transaction confirmations)
  • Background check services (for teacher verification)
  • Social media platforms (if you choose to connect)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

2.1 Platform Operations

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process bookings and payments
  • Facilitate communication between parents and teachers
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain platform security

2.2 Service Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features
  • Personalize your experience
  • Develop new services
  • Conduct research and analysis

2.3 Verification and Safety

  • Verify teacher identities and qualifications
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Ensure child safety
  • Enforce our Terms and policies

2.4 Communications

  • Send booking confirmations and reminders
  • Notify you of platform updates
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Respond to your inquiries

2.5 Legal Compliance

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Respond to legal requests
  • Protect our rights and interests

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal data. We share information only in these circumstances:

3.1 With Other Users

  • Parents can see: Teacher profiles, qualifications, reviews, availability
  • Teachers can see: Parent names, child information for booked sessions
  • Both can see: Session history, messages, reviews

3.2 With Service Providers

We share data with trusted third parties who help us operate the Platform:

  • Payment Processors: Paystack (for payment processing)
  • Cloud Hosting: Firebase (for data storage)
  • Email Services: For sending notifications
  • Analytics: To understand platform usage

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required to:

  • Comply with legal obligations (court orders, subpoenas)
  • Protect the safety of users or the public
  • Prevent fraud or illegal activity
  • Enforce our Terms and policies

3.4 Business Transfers

If Intellect Haven is acquired or merges with another company, your information may be transferred. We will notify you of such changes.

4. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

4.1 Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest
  • Access Controls: Role-based access to personal data
  • Firewalls: Network security to prevent unauthorized access
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and penetration testing

4.2 Organizational Safeguards

  • Staff training on data protection
  • Confidentiality agreements
  • Incident response procedures
  • Regular policy reviews

Your Responsibility

Protect your account by using a strong password, not sharing credentials, and logging out on shared devices.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary:

Data Type Retention Period
Account Information Duration of account + 30 days after deletion request
Booking Records 7 years (for tax and legal purposes)
Session Notes 3 years
Reviews Duration of teacher's presence on platform
Verification Documents Duration of teacher's presence + 1 year
Communication Logs 2 years
Usage Analytics Aggregated and anonymized after 2 years

6. Your Rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, you have the following rights:

6.1 Right to Access

You can request a copy of your personal data that we hold.

6.2 Right to Rectification

You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

6.3 Right to Erasure

You can request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations).

6.4 Right to Restrict Processing

You can request that we limit how we use your data.

6.5 Right to Data Portability

You can request your data in a portable format.

6.6 Right to Object

You can object to certain types of processing (e.g., marketing).

6.7 How to Exercise Your Rights

Contact us at privacy@intellecthaven.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Intellect Haven is designed for parents to find tutors for their children. We take special care with children's data:

  • Children's information is provided by parents/guardians
  • We collect only necessary information (name, age, learning needs)
  • Children's data is accessible only to their parents and booked teachers
  • We do not use children's data for marketing
  • Parents can delete their children's profiles at any time

Parental Control

Parents have full control over their children's information and can update or delete it at any time through their dashboard.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

8.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to remember your preferences and improve your experience.

8.2 Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose
Essential Cookies Required for platform functionality (login, security)
Preference Cookies Remember your settings and preferences
Analytics Cookies Help us understand how you use the platform

8.3 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect platform functionality.

9. Third-Party Services

Our Platform integrates with these third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Cloud database and authentication
  • Paystack: Payment processing
  • Google Fonts: Typography
  • Font Awesome: Icons

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside Ghana (e.g., where our cloud providers operate). We ensure:

  • Adequate data protection safeguards are in place
  • Compliance with Ghana's Data Protection Act
  • Contractual protections with service providers

Your continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
