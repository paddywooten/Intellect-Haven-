Cancellation Policy

Understanding our cancellation and refund policies

Overview

At Intellect Haven, we understand that circumstances can change. Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair to both parents and teachers while ensuring the platform runs smoothly.

Key Points

Free cancellation is available more than 24 hours before the session. Partial refunds are available 12-24 hours before. No refunds for cancellations less than 12 hours before or no-shows.

Cancellation Policy for Parents

Cancellation by Parents

If you need to cancel a booking, please do so as early as possible to allow teachers to fill the time slot.

Cancellation Time Refund Policy Teacher Compensation
More than 24 hours before session Full refund to parent No payment to teacher
12-24 hours before session 50% refund to parent Teacher receives 50% of booking fee
Less than 12 hours before session No refund Teacher receives full payment
No-show (parent doesn't attend) No refund Teacher receives full payment

How to Cancel

  • Go to "My Bookings" in your dashboard
  • Find the booking you want to cancel
  • Click "Cancel Booking"
  • Confirm the cancellation
  • You'll receive a confirmation email

Rescheduling

Instead of cancelling, you can also request to reschedule:

  • Contact the teacher directly through the platform
  • Agree on a new date and time
  • Cancel the original booking (subject to cancellation policy)
  • Create a new booking for the new time

Cancellation Policy for Teachers

Cancellation by Teachers

We understand that emergencies happen. However, frequent cancellations by teachers can damage trust and reputation on the platform.

Cancellation Time Parent Refund Teacher Penalty
More than 48 hours before session Full refund to parent No penalty
24-48 hours before session Full refund to parent Warning issued
Less than 24 hours before session Full refund + 10% credit to parent Account review + potential suspension
No-show (teacher doesn't attend) Full refund + 25% credit to parent Immediate account suspension

Important

Teachers who cancel frequently (3+ times in 30 days) may have their accounts suspended or permanently removed from the platform.

Emergency Situations

If you have an emergency and need to cancel last-minute:

  • Contact the parent immediately through the platform
  • Contact Intellect Haven support at support@intellecthaven.com
  • Provide documentation if possible (medical certificate, etc.)
  • Offer to reschedule at the parent's convenience

Refund Process

How Refunds Work

  • Refunds are processed automatically based on the cancellation timing
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method
  • Mobile Money refunds: Processed within 24 hours
  • Card refunds: May take 5-10 business days depending on your bank

Refund Timeline

Payment Method Processing Time
Mobile Money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo) Within 24 hours
Credit/Debit Cards 5-10 business days
Bank Transfer 3-5 business days

Dispute Resolution

If You Disagree with a Cancellation Decision

If you believe a cancellation was handled incorrectly:

  • Contact our support team within 7 days of the cancellation
  • Provide your booking ID and details of the issue
  • Include any relevant evidence (screenshots, messages, etc.)
  • Our team will review the case within 48 hours
  • We'll make a final decision based on the evidence

Mediation Process

For disputes between parents and teachers:

  • Both parties will be contacted for their perspective
  • Session notes and platform messages will be reviewed
  • A neutral mediator will make a fair decision
  • Decisions are final and binding

Special Circumstances

Force Majeure

In cases of extraordinary circumstances beyond anyone's control (natural disasters, government restrictions, etc.):

  • Full refunds will be issued to parents
  • Teachers will not be penalized
  • Bookings can be rescheduled at no cost

Technical Issues

If platform technical issues prevent a session from occurring:

  • Full refunds will be issued to parents
  • Teachers will receive compensation for their time
  • Sessions can be rescheduled at no cost

Quality Issues

If a parent is dissatisfied with the quality of tutoring:

  • Contact support within 24 hours of the session
  • Provide specific details about the quality concerns
  • Our team will investigate and may issue partial or full refunds
  • Teachers may receive warnings or account reviews

Policy Updates

Intellect Haven reserves the right to update this cancellation policy at any time. Changes will be:

  • Communicated via email to all users
  • Posted on our website with an updated date
  • Effective 30 days after notification

Continued use of the platform after policy changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

Questions?

If you have any questions about our cancellation policy, please don't hesitate to contact us.
