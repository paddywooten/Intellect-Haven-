Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Intellect Haven

General Questions

What is Intellect Haven?

Intellect Haven is Africa's trusted teacher-parent learning platform that connects verified teachers with parents for personalized, one-on-one tutoring sessions. We provide a safe, reliable marketplace where parents can find qualified tutors for their children's academic and extracurricular needs.

How does Intellect Haven work?

Our platform works in simple steps:

  • For Parents: Create an account, add your children's profiles, search for verified teachers, book sessions, and track progress
  • For Teachers: Create a profile, get verified, set your availability, receive booking requests, and conduct tutoring sessions
  • After each session, teachers submit lesson notes and parents can leave reviews
Is Intellect Haven free to use?

Creating an account and browsing teachers is completely free. You only pay when you book a tutoring session. Teachers set their own hourly rates, and Intellect Haven charges a small service fee to maintain the platform and ensure quality.

For Parents

How do I find the right teacher for my child?

Use our advanced search filters to find teachers by:

  • Subject (Mathematics, English, Science, etc.)
  • Curriculum (GES, British, Cambridge, IB, Montessori)
  • School level (Nursery, Primary, JHS, SHS)
  • Location (city and area)
  • Hourly rate range
  • Teacher rating and reviews

You can also read teacher profiles, view their qualifications, and check reviews from other parents.

Are all teachers verified?

Yes! All teachers on Intellect Haven go through a rigorous verification process that includes:

  • Government ID verification
  • Qualification certificate verification
  • Reference checks (optional but recommended)
  • Background screening

Only verified teachers can receive bookings. Look for the "Verified" badge on teacher profiles.

How do I book a session?

Booking a session is easy:

  • Find a teacher you like and click "View Profile"
  • Click "Book Session" on their profile
  • Select your child, subject, date, time, and duration
  • Add any notes about what you'd like covered
  • Review the total cost and confirm the booking

The teacher will receive your request and confirm it. You'll be notified once confirmed.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, you can cancel bookings based on our cancellation policy:

  • Free cancellation: More than 24 hours before the session
  • 50% refund: 12-24 hours before the session
  • No refund: Less than 12 hours before the session or no-show

To cancel, go to "My Bookings" and click "Cancel" on the booking you want to cancel.

How do I track my child's progress?

After each completed session, teachers submit detailed session notes including:

  • Lesson topic covered
  • What was taught
  • Homework assigned
  • Student progress and performance

You can view these notes in your "Session History" page. You can also leave reviews for teachers after completed sessions.

For Teachers

How do I become a teacher on Intellect Haven?

To become a teacher on Intellect Haven:

  • Click "Sign Up" and select "Teacher" as your user type
  • Complete your profile with your qualifications, experience, and subjects
  • Upload verification documents (ID and qualification certificates)
  • Set your hourly rate and availability
  • Wait for admin verification (usually within 48 hours)
  • Once verified, you can start receiving booking requests
How much can I earn?

Your earnings depend on:

  • Your hourly rate (you set this)
  • Number of sessions you conduct
  • Session duration

Intellect Haven charges a 15% service fee on each booking. For example, if you charge GHS 50/hour for a 2-hour session (GHS 100 total), you'll receive GHS 85 after the service fee.

Top teachers on our platform earn between GHS 2,000 - GHS 8,000 per month depending on their availability and reputation.

How do I get paid?

Payments are processed through Mobile Money (MoMo):

  • Parents pay when they book a session
  • Funds are held securely until the session is completed
  • After you mark the session as complete and submit session notes, payment is released to your MoMo account within 24 hours
  • You can track all your earnings in your "Earnings" dashboard
What happens if a parent cancels?

If a parent cancels a booking:

  • More than 24 hours before: Full refund to parent, no payment to you
  • 12-24 hours before: 50% refund to parent, you receive 50% of the booking fee
  • Less than 12 hours or no-show: No refund to parent, you receive full payment

You'll be notified immediately when a cancellation occurs.

How do I set my availability?

To set your availability:

  • Go to your dashboard and click "Availability"
  • Toggle on the days you're available
  • Select your available time slots for each day (8 AM - 8 PM)
  • Save your availability

Parents can only book sessions during your available times. You can update your availability anytime.

Payments & Billing

What payment methods are accepted?

We currently accept:

  • Mobile Money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo)
  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard) - coming soon
  • Bank Transfer - coming soon

All payments are processed securely through Paystack.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes! We take security seriously:

  • All payments are processed through Paystack, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor
  • We never store your card details on our servers
  • All data is encrypted using industry-standard SSL encryption
  • We comply with Ghana's Data Protection Act
What is the service fee?

Intellect Haven charges a 15% service fee on each booking. This fee covers:

  • Platform maintenance and development
  • Teacher verification and quality assurance
  • Customer support
  • Payment processing
  • Dispute resolution

The service fee is automatically deducted from the teacher's earnings.

Safety & Trust

How do you ensure child safety?

Child safety is our top priority. We ensure safety through:

  • Rigorous teacher verification: ID checks, qualification verification, and reference checks
  • Background screening: All teachers undergo background checks
  • Review system: Parents can leave reviews after each session
  • Session notes: Teachers must submit detailed notes after each session
  • Reporting system: Easy reporting of any concerns
  • Admin monitoring: Our team monitors platform activity
What if I have a problem with a teacher or parent?

If you experience any issues:

  • Try to resolve the issue directly through respectful communication
  • If that doesn't work, contact our support team at support@intellecthaven.com
  • Provide details of the issue, including booking ID and any relevant information
  • Our team will investigate and mediate the situation
  • We may issue refunds, suspend accounts, or take other appropriate action

We take all complaints seriously and aim to resolve issues within 48 hours.

How do you protect my personal data?

We protect your data through:

  • Compliance with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)
  • Industry-standard encryption for all data
  • Secure cloud storage with regular backups
  • Limited access to personal information (only what's necessary)
  • Clear privacy policy explaining how we use your data
  • Option to delete your account and data at any time

Read our full Privacy Policy for more details.

Technical Support

I forgot my password. What should I do?

To reset your password:

  • Click "Login" in the top navigation
  • Click "Forgot password?" link
  • Enter your email address
  • Check your email for a password reset link
  • Click the link and create a new password

The reset link expires in 1 hour for security. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

The website isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try these troubleshooting steps:

  • Refresh the page (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R)
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  • Check your internet connection
  • Disable browser extensions temporarily
  • Make sure you're using an updated browser

If the problem persists, contact our support team with details of the issue.

Can I use Intellect Haven on my mobile phone?

Yes! Intellect Haven is fully responsive and works on all devices:

  • Desktop computers
  • Laptops
  • Tablets
  • Smartphones (iOS and Android)

Our mobile apps are coming soon! For now, you can use the website through your mobile browser. We recommend adding it to your home screen for quick access.
